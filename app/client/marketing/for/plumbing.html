What it does

Built for the way plumbing work actually runs.

The money leak in plumbing is rarely the wrench work — it is the unbilled trip, the forgotten part, the invoice sent four days late. Crewkat keeps the paperwork inside the call: open the job when you roll up, and the estimate, the photos, and the invoice are already where the work happened.

  • Service calls as proper jobs. Every call gets a job record with the client, the address, and the problem — so the second visit to the same house starts from the first visit's notes, not from scratch.
  • Estimates from the truck. Price the repair on the spot, with options where there is a choice — repair or replace, standard or upgraded fixture — ticked and approved by the client on the estimate.
  • Rough and final, tracked. On bigger work, run the job in stages: rough-in, inspection, final. Daily logs and photos keep each stage documented before the walls close over it.
  • Photos before it gets covered. The valve behind the wall, the line in the trench, the serial plate on the heater — photographed and filed to the job, findable the next time anyone opens that wall.
  • Fast invoicing, same visit. Convert the approved estimate to an invoice in one tap before you leave. With Pay Now on Premium, the client pays by card or bank transfer from the invoice link while the tools are still warm.
  • A service history per client. Every call, job, and invoice for a client — across every address they own — stays on their record in the client portal system, so regulars are treated like regulars.

Common questions

Plumbing, answered.

Is Crewkat worth it for small service calls?

That is what it is built for. A call takes a couple of minutes to open as a job, the estimate and invoice come off the same record, and the paperwork closes the same day instead of piling up.

Can I track rough-in and final stages on bigger jobs?

Yes. Run the job in stages with daily logs, documents, and photos filed to each stage, and photograph rough work before it is covered so the record survives the drywall.

How do clients pay?

With Premium, the invoice link carries Pay Now: card or bank transfer (ACH) from the link, recorded automatically. Or take a check or cash as usual and record the payment against the invoice.
