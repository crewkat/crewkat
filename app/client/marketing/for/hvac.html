What it does

Built for the way hvac work actually runs.

When the first heat wave hits, the companies that win are the ones whose records are already in order. Crewkat keeps a service history for every system you touch — equipment photos, notes, repairs, and invoices — and puts maintenance visits on the schedule so they happen on time, every season.

  • Service history per system. Photograph the data plate, log the model and the work done, and file it to the client's record. The next tech — or you, two years later — opens the job and knows the system before the panel comes off.
  • Seasonal maintenance, scheduled. Build maintenance plans the practical way: recurring visits on the job schedule and recurring invoices to match, so spring and fall tune-ups are booked work, not a scramble.
  • Repair or replace, approved in writing. Price both paths as options on the estimate — the repair, the new system, the upgraded filtration — and let the client tick and approve the one they choose.
  • Install documentation, complete. New system photos, line sets, pads, permits paperwork, and the old unit's haul-off — logged on the job, with load records for what left the site and what it cost.
  • Daily logs across the install. Multi-day installs get a dated log and photos per day, which roll into a client-ready work summary at handover — what was installed and what was done, in one link.
  • Get paid from the invoice link. Convert the job to an invoice in a tap. With Pay Now on Premium, clients pay by card or bank transfer from the link — handy on big-ticket installs with a balance due at startup.

Repair or replace, without the fog

Both prices, one signature.

The hardest HVAC conversation is the honest one: this repair buys a year, a new system buys fifteen. Estimate options put both numbers in front of the client in writing, with the extras priced separately — so the decision, whichever way it goes, is documented and nobody re-litigates it at the next breakdown.

Common questions

HVAC, answered.

How do maintenance plans work in Crewkat?

As scheduled work: recurring maintenance visits go on the job schedule, and recurring invoices can match the plan's billing. The visits land on the client's service history as they happen, so the plan and the record stay together.

Can I keep a history for each system a client has?

Yes. Each job records the equipment photos, notes, and work for that visit against the client and address, so a house with two systems builds a history you can open on any later call.

How do clients pay for a big install balance?

With Premium, the invoice link carries Pay Now — card or bank transfer (ACH) from the link, recorded automatically against the job. Deposits and progress payments record the same way.
