What it does

Your contract. Your terms. Signed today.

Crewkat doesn't force you into a generic template. Bring the agreement your attorney already approved, and Crewkat handles the signing: field placement, the client link, the dated signature, and the finished document.

  • Tap-to-place fields. Drop signature, date, and fill-in fields exactly where they belong on your pages — sized the way you want them.
  • Clients sign from a link. No account, no app, no printer. They review, fill in what's asked, draw their signature, and they're done.
  • A fresh signature every time. Signatures are drawn at the moment of signing — never stored and reused — so a signature on a Crewkat document means someone actually signed it.
  • One true signed copy. The signed PDF is composed once, on the original pages, and it's the same file everywhere: your records, the client's copy, and the email. It files itself to the job's documents.
  • Change orders included. Scope changes get the same treatment — written, sent, and countersigned from the client's link instead of agreed over a handshake.

How it works

From PDF to signed in three steps.

1

Upload and sign yours

Add your contract PDF to the job, draw your signature once at setup, and your stamp is on the document.

2

Place the client's fields

Tap to place the client's signature and date fields on the right page, in the right spot. Add fill-in fields — initials, a checkbox, a date — where your contract asks for them.

3

Send the link

Text or email the link. Your client signs on their phone, and the finished, signed PDF appears in the job — with a copy emailed to them automatically.

Why it matters

The signature is the job's green light.

A signed contract before demo day protects your deposit, your schedule, and your scope. Crewkat makes signing the easiest step of the job instead of the one that drags through a week of "we'll print it tonight." Status is visible at a glance: not sent, link opened, viewed, signed.

Change orders without the fight

Extra work, agreed in writing.

When the client wants the wall moved or the fixture upgraded, send a change order through their portal link. They approve and sign before the work happens — so "you never told me it cost extra" never gets said at your final invoice.

Common questions

Contracts, answered.

Do I have to use a Crewkat contract template?

No. Upload the contract you already use — Crewkat adds the signing to your document instead of replacing it. You can also build a document from pasted text when you don't have a PDF handy.

Does my client need an account to sign?

No. They open your link, review the pages, fill in the fields you placed, and draw their signature. The signed copy is emailed to them and stored on the job.

What about change orders?

Change orders are documents too: describe the change, price it, send it through the client link, and it needs the client's signature before it's part of the job.
