What it does

A quote that sells the upgrade for you.

The best time to offer the better tile, the extra outlet, or the extended warranty is on the estimate, in writing — not mid-job with dust in the air. Crewkat estimates carry a base scope plus clearly priced options your client selects with a tap.

  • Optional add-ons with tick-box approval. Add upgrades as separate priced options; the client ticks what they want and the total recalculates instantly.
  • Only accepted options get billed. When you convert the estimate to an invoice, the base scope plus accepted options flow in — declined options stay out. No disputes about what was agreed.
  • Materials buy list. Turn a won estimate into a materials list so you know what to order before the first trip to the supply house.
  • Professional PDFs. Clean templates with your business details, grouped scope sections, and a clear total — the kind of quote that wins against a number scribbled on a card.
  • Fast to build. Line items with quantities, rates, discounts, and tax; duplicate a past estimate when a similar job comes in.

Common questions

Estimates, answered.

How do optional add-ons work?

Mark any line as an option with its own price. On the client's link, options show as tick boxes: ticking one adds it to the total. When you invoice, only the options the client accepted are included.

Can the estimate become my materials order?

Yes. A won estimate generates a materials buy list from its line items, so ordering for the job starts from what was actually sold.

Do estimates cost extra?

Estimates and invoices share the free plan's 3 documents a month; Premium is unlimited for $19/month. Details on pricing.
